# PokeBonk — Plan Your Next Run

PokeBonk is a free browser survival game with 151 Kanto Pokémon. Choose a League partner, dodge hordes, collect experience and build around attacks, vitamins and items. Evolve during a run, combine eligible attacks at Bill’s Machine and spend Stardust on permanent Lab upgrades between attempts.

[PokeBonk.me](https://pokebonk.me/) is an independent English fan guide. The game, accounts and saves are at [pokebonk.com](https://pokebonk.com/?lang=en).

## Common questions

### Is PokeBonk free?

Yes. The Play page opens the original free browser game. This fan site does not host the game or request your game login.

### Can every Pokémon start a run?

Only 79 are selectable League partners. Other forms belong to evolution paths. Use the Pokédex League filter when comparing starting kits.

### How do fusions work?

Both eligible ingredients need level 8 at Bill’s Machine. Check [fusion entries](https://pokebonk.me/wiki/attacks#fusions) for the exact pair. Signature recipes take priority over reactions, same-type outcomes and MissingNo. Weather, booster and existing fusion attacks cannot be ingredients.

### What should I buy with Stardust?

Identify whether health, capacity or upgrade offers limit the run. The [Lab calculator](https://pokebonk.me/wiki/lab#calculator) totals the actual prices between levels instead of multiplying the first-level price.

### Is the tier list based on win rates?

No. It compares solo Classic openings, weighting comfort, offensive potential and utility. Each partner has its own explanation and weakness. Survival scaling, co-op roles and a particular build can change your preference.

### Do Mastery Looks require solo play?

Most now count in solo or co-op, with each player responsible for their conditions. Hit-limit challenges remain solo-only. Read each goal; Survival and Tower wins are not Classic wins.

### Are standings live?

No. The displayed boards were recorded on October 28, 2026, from v0.8.62. Projected prizes follow the placement rules. Open the game for subsequent positions and final season results.

### Where are my choices saved?

Tier placements, achievement marks and vitamin plans stay in this browser’s localStorage. They do not sync with the game or other devices. Clearing site data removes them; the tier board supports JSON export.
